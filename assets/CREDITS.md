CarRace changes: normalized orientation and scale, crease-aware smooth shading, new paint/glass/tire/rim materials, original fictional liveries and numbers projected onto the body, driver window nets, distance-driven wheel animation, and original rendered preview images. The unmodified source is retained in `source/stock-camry-ns.glb`; the refined runtime model is `cars/stock-car.glb`. `tools/prepare-car.py` applies 6 mm body-edge bevels and weighted panel normals in Blender.

The source model is described by its author as a low-poly NASCAR-style stock car. The game uses fictional drivers and original team graphics. No official team sponsor artwork or driver photographs are included. CarRace is independent of NASCAR and vehicle manufacturers.
